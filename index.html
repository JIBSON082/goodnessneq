The book

Being good is a foundation. Not the whole plan.

Goodness Is Not a Strategy is a practical personal-development book about bringing character and competence together.

You can remain kind while learning to be discerning. You can hold on to your values while becoming more capable. You can be a good person and still learn to make difficult decisions, take calculated risks, and pursue what matters to you with intention.

01

Being kind doesn't mean you should have no boundaries.

02

Being humble doesn't mean you should make yourself small.

03

Having faith doesn't mean you should abandon preparation.

04

Good intentions don't mean you automatically know how to achieve good outcomes.

You don't have to become a different person to build a better life.

You need to become a more intentional version of yourself.
